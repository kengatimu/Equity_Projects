PROJECT DESCRIPTION
DPD (days past due) is an indicator of a loan that has not been repaid/settled by the due date. For every day that lapses beyond DPD, charges and fees maybe incurred that the customer has to bear for late repayment.
echoCloud needs to get a daily sync (and therefore archive) of all loan accounts that have a DPD > 1.
It is designed for consumption by other services (e.g. Experian).
Finserve Finacle will maintain a Daily time-based batch job that would ideally be run after End of Day processes.

PROJECT REQUIREMENT FROM FINACLE PERSPECTIVE
1. Send Bank Id and Loan Foracid
2. Get Loan Status: 
			ACCOUNT CLOSURE FLAG
			BALANCE
			LAST PAYMENT DATE
			PAYOFF DATE

PROJECT HANDLING

A. QUERY PREPARATION
	1. GETTING BALANCE AND CLOSURE FLAG
			select clr_bal_amt balance,acct_cls_flg closerflag from tbaadm.gam where bank_id = '54' and foracid= '1460563444511' and schm_type='LAA'  
			
	2.  GETTING LAST PAYMENT DATE
			select max(last_adj_date) lastpayment from tbaadm.ldt where bank_id= '54' and  last_adj_date is not null 
			and acid in (select acid from tbaadm.gam where bank_id= '54' and foracid ='1460563444511') group by acid
			
	3. GETTING PAYOFF DATE
			select payoff_date payoff from tbaadm.lam where bank_id='54' and acid in (select acid from tbaadm.gam where foracid ='1460563444511')
