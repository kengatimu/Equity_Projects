China Details:

A. AliPay

Prerequisite
1. Sender confirms the exact name of the beneficiary in pinyin, and beneficiary mobile number (MSISDN) with country code
    before the start of the trasaction

Quote:
1. Bank officer enters beneficiary mobile number (MSISDN) with country code, in destination.accountNumber field
2. Bank officer enters bicWallet value as AliPay in the destination.bicWallet field
3. Bank officer enters CHN as destination.countryCode value
4. Bank officer enters CNY as destination.currency value

Transaction
A. Receiver Details
    1. Bank officer enters the name (in pinyin) in the receiver.lastname and receiver.firstname field
    2. Bank officer enters the beneficiary mobile number in the receiver.mobileNumber field
    
B. Sender Details
    1. Bank officer enters the sender firstname and lastname in the sender.firstName and sender.lastName fields
    2. Bank officer enters the sender date of birth in the sender.dateOfBirth field
    3. Bank officer enters the sender mobile number in the in the sender.mobileNumber field
    4. Bank officer enters the sender id number in the sender.email field   
    5. Bank officer enters the sender id type in the sender.nationality field  
    6. Bank officer enters the sender address:
        i. Sender address needs the following fields: 
            sender.address.city
            sender.address.line1
            sender.address.postalcode
            

B. UnionPay

Prerequisite
1. Sender confirms the exact name of the beneficiary in pinyin, Id number, and bank account number (bank account number = Union Pay Card Number)
    before the start of the trasaction

Quote:
1. Bank officer enters UnionPay as the bicWallet value for destination.bicWallet field
2. Bank officer enters the beneficiary bank account number (Union Pay Card Number) in the destination.accountNumber field
3. Bank officer enters CHN as destination.countryCode value
4. Bank officer enters CNY as destination.currency value

Transaction
A. Receiver Details
    1. Bank officer enters the name (in pinyin) in the receiver.lastname and receiver.firstname field
    2. Bank officer enters the beneficiary id number in the orgReceiver.registrationNumber field
    3. Bank officer enters the beneficiary bank account number (Union Pay Card Number) in the destination.accountNumber fiels
    3. Bank officer enters the beneficiary mobile number in the receiver.mobileNumber field
    
B. Sender Details
    1. Bank officer enters the sender firstname and lastname in the sender.firstName and sender.lastName fields
    2. Bank officer enters the sender date of birth in the sender.dateOfBirth field
    3. Bank officer enters the sender mobile number in the in the sender.mobileNumber field
    4. Bank officer enters the sender  id number in the sender.email field
