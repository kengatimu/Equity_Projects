CREDIT TRANSFER FLOW

Assuming you are sending a request from Telkom to Telkom (1001)

1. You send a credit transfer request to IPS through endpoint: 

https://api.start.ipsl.co.ke/iso20022/async/v1/credit-transfer

2. IPS forwards the credit transfer(pacs.008) request to beneficiary participant by calling the participant's credit transfer endpoint:

e.g. https://exploretest.telkom.co.ke/v1/credit-transfer

3. Beneficiary processes the request and responds to IPS with a payment status report (pacs.002) to the endpoint:

https://api.start.ipsl.co.ke/iso20022/async/v1/payment-status-report

4. IPS receives the pacs.002 response and formulates final pacs.002 responses to both originator and beneficiary through the participant endpoints:

originator: https://exploretest.telkom.co.ke/v1/payment-status-report
beneficiary: https://exploretest.telkom.co.ke/v1/payment-status-report

If you are acting as both originator and beneficiary, you will receive two final pacs.002 responses from IPS (Of course this is just for testing purposes. In the actual production system, this may not make much sense)
